Get from the bedroom to another bedroom.

I start in an ordinary bedroom. My way out is the open door — I drive to it and pass through into
the hallway.

Just outside, the doors are really close together: I take the first door on my right, which opens
into the second bedroom.

Inside the second bedroom there is a bed. I approach it — not too close — and stop on the open floor
near the bed. That is where I'm done.
