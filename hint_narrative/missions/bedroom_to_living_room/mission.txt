I start in an ordinary bedroom. My way out is the open door — I drive to it, pass through, and turn
left into the hallway.

The hallway has a few doors along it, but one open passage that is not framed by a door leads to the
living room. I follow the hallway to that open living-room entrance and cross through it.

Inside is an ordinary living room with a couch somewhere on the open floor. I enter, drive up to the
couch, and stop on the clear floor in front of it. That is where I'm done.
